# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Anyone that might be a potential scam victim.

## What exactly is going wrong?
A high number of scam victims are struggling to distinguish legitimate messages from scams as scammers use convincing language and create a sense of urgency.

## Why does it matter?
This system helps every people of age to detect scam before the disaster itself happens. Nowadays, there are variety of tactics that scammers use which involves psychological manipulation and not everyone is cybersecurity-aware to judge the other person that are communicating online with them. Hence, it goes beyond simply labelling a message as a scam but also open their minds to a whole new knowledge.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
The increasing prevalence of scam messages including phishing, banking scams and fake job offers makes it difficult for users to distinguish fraudulent messages from legitimate ones. Existing automated classification approaches may identify suspicious messages but provide limited explanations of the reasons behind their predictions. Therefore, this project proposes an intelligent scam risk assessment system that combines Machine Learning, rule-based indicators and Large Language Models (LLMs) to classify suspicious text messages, identify potential warning signs and provide understandable explanations to help users make more informed decisions.
